Gameplay

One Game, Phone by Phone

Every player uses their own phone. Here is a seven-player game from Frank’s seat. He drew Merlin.

01

Gather

Start a room on one phone. Friends join with a six-letter code.

02

Set the Table

The host picks roles. Suggested lineups for 5–10 players.

03

Draw a Role

Each phone flips a private card: who you are, who you can see.

04

Night Falls

The host phone narrates. Merlin sees evil, except Mordred.

05

Debate

Speak in turn around the table while the leader drafts a team.

06

Vote

Everyone approves or rejects the team. Five rejections and evil wins.

07

Quest

Sealed cards. Here quest 4 needs two fails, and one saboteur isn’t enough.

08

Unmask

The Assassin gets one guess at Merlin, then every role is revealed.
